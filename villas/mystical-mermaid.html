Mystical Mermaid

Waterfront Whimsical Villa in St. John, USVI

2 BR | 2 BA | Sleeps 4

About Mystical Mermaid

Mystical Mermaid is a consistently rated 5-star luxury stone villa, tastefully updated in Caribbean colors and finished to the highest standards. Close to world-famous beaches. Features 2 interior bedrooms, 2 baths, salt water pool and hot tub with mountain views. The owner's suite has a king bed, AC, satellite TV, and large ensuite bathroom. The second bedroom has two twin beds (can become a king), AC, and ensuite bathroom. Open kitchen, living room and dining room with high-band WiFi throughout and 2 desks for remote work. Pool veranda opens off the living, dining and owner's suite. Surrounded by lush gardens and Bismarckia palms.
