Rainbow Beach House

Beachfront Villa in St. John, USVI

4 BR | 4 BA | Sleeps 8

Overview

Beds 4 Baths 4 Sleeps 8

Rainbow Beach House is a beautiful 4 bedroom 4 bath beachfront home with a pool and pool spa. The home was completely renovated in 2018 and when you enter the villa, you will be welcomed by the warm and inviting atmosphere of this lovely home, you may even find you never want to leave.

The home is a two level design all with interior access, perfect for families and/or couples traveling together. The great room is an open floor plan, with the kitchen, living and dining area being the centerpiece of this beautiful home. There is a 24-foot wall of screened sliding glass doors that open on to the pool and pool deck, all which enjoy the stunning views of Johnson Bay and beyond. On opposite sides of the great room are two bedrooms, the master bedroom (king) is on the left and the upper guest bedroom (queen) is on the right. Both have screened sliders that open on to the pool deck and each include private baths with tiled showers. The lower level bedrooms are accessed via a spiral staircase. The lower left bedroom (king) and lower right bedroom (queen) each have a private bath with shower and are connected by a common hallway. From the lower bedrooms there are screened sliding glass doors which open to the beach level. You can easily walk out your door to go swimming and snorkeling. If you like more activity, you can rent paddle boards and kayaks during your stay, if rented for the week most companies will deliver them to the villa for your stay.

No matter what time of year you travel, you are sure to always enjoy the sounds of the surf and the overall relaxing, beach front atmosphere of this tropical vacation home.
