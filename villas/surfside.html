Surfside

Oceanfront Family Villa in St. John, USVI

3 BR | 3 BA | Sleeps 6

Overview

Beds 3 Baths 3 Sleeps 6

Surfside is a welcoming 3-bedroom oceanfront villa designed with families in mind. Located on St. John's beautiful north shore, this villa offers easy access to some of the Caribbean's most celebrated beaches including Trunk Bay, Cinnamon Bay, and Maho Bay. The spacious living area opens to a large pool deck with panoramic ocean views, making it easy to keep an eye on the kids while relaxing in comfort. Each bedroom is air-conditioned with its own bathroom, ensuring privacy for everyone. The fully equipped kitchen makes meal prep a breeze, and the outdoor grill is perfect for evening barbecues. Surfside combines the comforts of home with the beauty of a St. John oceanfront location.

Property Details

Distance To Town: It's a 15-18 minute drive to the shops, restaurants, and grocery stores in Cruz Bay.

Living And Dining: The cathedral ceilings, rose tiled floors, and rattan furniture create a tropical atmosphere in the intimate living area. A stereo/cassette/CD player, cable color HDTV, VCR, and DVD player supply the electronic entertainment. Surfside offers two dining locations, both with romantic ocean views. The interior teak dining table seats 6 comfortably, as does the beautiful outdoor teak dining table located on the oceanfront deck.

Kitchen: The cook's kitchen features brand new marble countertops, a farm sink, and professional stainless steel appliances, including a four burner dual-fuel stove, refrigerator, dishwasher, microwave, and all cooking and dining accessories. A gas barbeque is located on the patio, and the washer and dryer are located on the lower level.

Bedroom Details: All three bedrooms are similar in size and furnishings and all offer good privacy, air conditioning, and great ocean views. The bedroom on the main level has a queen bed and French doors that open to a small deck. The upper bedroom has a king bed and a small deck with lovely views. The lower level bedroom has a king bed and is off the pool deck. Each bedroom has an attractive adjoining tiled bath and shower.

Vehicle Information: A 4 Wheel Drive vehicle is needed for travel on St. John.

Phone Service/Cell Phones: Local phone service is interrupted regularly, and in spite of our efforts to reinstate service it can sometimes take weeks. AT&T is the only cell service we find operates efficiently on the island. If you do not have AT&T, we strongly suggest you bring a pay as you go AT&T cell phone with you, as we do not provide cell phones should the local phone service be interrupted.

Internet Service: The internet service is subject to frequent interruption. We cannot guarantee internet quality or speed. Renters might find it convenient to travel with a cellular carrier MiFi; a couple of examples are AT&T and Virgin Mobile. There will be no refunds due to the internet not working.

Pool Floats: Pool floats are not guaranteed with your villa rental. If you want access to them, please bring them with you.
