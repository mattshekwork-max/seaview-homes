Waterfall

Hillside Pool Villa in St. John, USVI

3 BR | 3.5 BA | Sleeps 6

About Waterfall

Waterfall is a destination villa that guests often find difficult to leave. There's the enduring lure of the gorgeous National Park beaches and beautiful views of Fish Bay and beyond — all the way to St. Croix. Sit beneath the waterfall in one of St. John's largest solar-heated, infinity-edge swimming pools and feel the tranquility. After an invigorating workout in the air-conditioned gym, relax in the poolside six-person spa while you contemplate dining under the stars in the romantic dining gazebo. Designed for easy living, two bedrooms have inside access on the same level as the pool and spa, and all three equal-sized bedrooms offer beautiful water views and ensuite baths. An entry ramp from the driveway simplifies access for those who find stairs difficult, a fully equipped air-conditioned office with whole-house WiFi keeps you connected, and a 28-kilowatt automatic generator keeps everything running smoothly through any power outage. Children ages 8 and up welcome.
