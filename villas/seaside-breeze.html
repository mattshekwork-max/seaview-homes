Seaside Breeze

Beachfront Villa in Coral Bay, St. John, USVI

4 BR | 3 BA | Sleeps 8

Overview

Beds 4 Baths 3 Sleeps 8

Seaside Breeze is a spacious and easy to love beachfront villa that was added to our villa portfolio in May, 2021, after being purchased by a new owner. The house was built in 2006, and it has not been previously offered for rentals. From 2006 - 2021 Seaside Breeze was lightly used and was very well maintained by its original owner, resulting in a home that is currently in very fine condition. With 3700 sq/ft of interior space and 1740 sq/ft of exterior decks, Seaside Breeze is a roomy villa that will very comfortably accommodate 8 guests in 4 a/c bedrooms with 3 baths.

Seaside Breeze's design incorporates some unique features, including floor to ceiling windows with two huge trapezoid windows on the front and back (east and west) sides of the house. The oversized windows combine with coral stone tiled floors, an open floor plan and cedar woodwork to produce a cheerful, attractive and alluring interior. The great room includes the blue tooth enabled audio system has 8 speakers that will distribute lively (or soothing) music throughout the great room, or to a selected zone. Seaside Breeze is insulated, which is unusual in St. John homes, and this helps keep the house comfy and cool.

Seaside Breeze's single best feature is probably its location. You are just a few steps from a sandy beach and the 82 degree, crystal clear waters of the Caribbean - where you can swim, snorkel, kayak, paddle board or go boating - in your front yard.

Villa Details

Distance To Town: Located in Coral Bay; 25 minute drive to Cruz Bay.

Living And Dining: With its bold 30 foot cathedral ceiling, huge trapezoid windows running fore and aft, and its wide expanse of white coral stone floors, the great room of Seaside Breeze is pretty to look at and even better to live in. The living room opens to the deck and is located on the left side of the great room. It contains a 50" TV with surround sound, plus a stereo system with blue tooth and 8 zoned speakers that are placed throughout the great room. There are four dining areas, with the primary dining room located on the right side of the great room and also opening to the pool deck. The gas BBQ grill is conveniently located just outside the interior dining room, and almost within reach of the dining table on the deck. There is a third dining table in the middle of the great room, and a mahogany bar off the kitchen also has seating for six.

Kitchen: The little used kitchen is clean, modern and very well equipped for home cooked meals, or if you prefer, hire a private chef to treat you to some culinary delights. The kitchen includes mahogany counter-tops, with white cabinets and drawers filled with cookware and dinnerware. Stainless steel appliances include a cooktop stove and oven and a refrigerator/freezer with water and ice dispensers, a microwave and a toaster oven, coffeemaker and other gadgets and kitchen tools. A washer/dryer is conveniently located within the kitchen.

Bedroom Details: The home has 4 bedrooms all with A/C and 42" TVs. There is a master bedroom on the main level with a king bed and attached bathroom with tiled shower. The additional three bedrooms are on the upper level and are accessed by an interior staircase. The second master bedroom has a king bed with an attached bath and tiled shower. There are two guest bedrooms which share a full bath. One is a king and the second is two twins.

Vehicle Information: A 4 Wheel Drive vehicle is needed for travel on St John.

Rates, Printing Errors, Changes and Omissions: Caribbean Villas is not responsible for printing errors and inadvertent omissions. All rates and villa details are subject to change without prior notice.

Phone Service/Cell Phones: Local phone service is interrupted regularly and in spite of our efforts to reinstate service it can sometimes take weeks. AT&T is the only cell service we find that operates efficiently on the island. If you do not have AT&T we strongly suggest you bring a pay-as-you-go AT&T cell phone with you as we do not provide cell phones should the local phone service be interrupted.

Internet Service: The internet service is subject to frequent interruption. We cannot guarantee internet quality or speed. Renters might find it convenient to travel with a cellular carrier MiFi - a couple of examples are AT&T and Virgin Mobile. There will be no refunds due to the internet not working.

Pool Floats: Pool floats are not guaranteed with your villa rental. If you want access to them, please bring them with you.
