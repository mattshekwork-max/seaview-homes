Experience the Magic of St. John

Curated Island Activities

Beyond the villa, the entire island is your playground.

Trunk Bay

Located within the Virgin Islands National Park, Trunk Bay was named one of the top 10 beaches in the world by Condé Nast Traveler. Its white-sand shores, turquoise waters, and lush green backdrop make it a must-see. Don't miss the famous underwater snorkeling trail.

Best for: Snorkeling, Photography, Families

Honeymoon Beach

Honeymoon Beach offers impressive mountain views of the North Shore. Rent a kayak or paddleboard and snorkel in the clear, calm water. Accessible by trail or a short taxi from Caneel Bay. Perfect for a quieter beach day.

Best for: Kayaking, Paddleboarding, Couples

Hawksnest Bay

A beautiful North Shore beach within the National Park. Hawksnest offers reefs to snorkel, restrooms, picnic tables, and a shaded picnic area. It catches plenty of morning sun and offers wonderful sunsets. A reef a short swim away and shallow waters make it ideal for families.

Best for: Snorkeling, Picnics, Sunsets

Virgin Islands National Park

Two-thirds of St. John is owned by the Virgin Islands National Park, making it the most undeveloped of the three US Virgin Islands. Hiking trails crisscross the island, leading to historic sugar plantations and the popular Reef Bay Trail. Most villa rentals are within minutes of North Shore beaches and trailheads.

Best for: Hiking, History, Nature Photography

Cruz Bay Dining

From high-end seafood to authentic Caribbean street food, the dining scene in St. John is vibrant. Visit the local eateries in Cruz Bay for a taste of the island's fresh catch, Caribbean fusion, and laid-back island atmosphere.

Best for: Foodies, Culture, Evening Escapes

Sailing & Water Sports

Rent a private catamaran or explore the coastline by kayak. The surrounding waters are teeming with marine life, making it a premier destination for divers and sailors. Charter a boat for a full-day sail around the island or a sunset cruise.

Best for: Groups, Exploration, Adventure
