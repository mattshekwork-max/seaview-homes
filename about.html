About Seaview Homes

Four Decades of St. John Hospitality

Locally owned. Personally managed. Deeply rooted in the island.

Seaview Homes was established on St. John in 1986 — long before the island became the sought-after destination it is today. What started as a small, family-run operation has grown into a trusted portfolio of one to five-bedroom villas, ranging from affordable island getaways to luxurious oceanfront estates.


We don't just rent villas — we share our home. Our broad knowledge of the island and its activities means we can point you to the best snorkeling spots, the quietest beaches, the best local restaurants, and the trails most visitors never find. We're here to serve you, and we mean it.


From the moment you arrive at the ferry terminal to the day you reluctantly pack your bags, our team makes sure your stay is effortless. We handle the details — airport pickups, grocery stocking, activity bookings — so you can focus on what brought you here: the beauty of St. John.

Why St. John?

Untouched Beauty

Two-thirds of St. John is protected as the Virgin Islands National Park — making it the most undeveloped of the three US Virgin Islands. Pristine beaches, hiking trails, and historic sugar plantations are all minutes from our villas.

Quiet & Uncrowded

Less commercialized than St. Thomas or St. Croix, St. John maintains a quiet, unhurried atmosphere. No traffic lights, no chain resorts — just you and the island.

World-Class Snorkeling & Diving

Trunk Bay's underwater snorkeling trail, the reefs at Hawksnest, and the marine life around Honeymoon Beach make St. John a premier destination for water lovers.
